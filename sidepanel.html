Automated scanning of Instagram carries some account risk. Delays are kept deliberately generous (2-5 s between requests, longer pauses every few batches), so a big account can take a long time. That is intended. Everything stays in this browser.

Import from Instagram data export

        
No network calls and no rate limits. In Instagram: Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information → choose Followers and following, format JSON. Select followers_1.json (plus followers_2.json ... if present) and following.json below.

        
Limitation: the export has no numeric user IDs, so matching is by username. Someone who renamed their account since the export may be listed wrongly.
